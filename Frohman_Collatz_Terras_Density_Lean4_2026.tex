\begin{abstract}
We present a zero-\texttt{sorry}, 7-module Lean 4 formalization (\texttt{TerrasDensity/Basic.lean}, \texttt{Covering.lean}, \texttt{Density.lean}, \texttt{Inclusion.lean}, \texttt{Tail.lean}) of the finite-prefix Terras parity-vector stopping-time density structure for the $3n+1$ map. Here \texttt{FiniteStoppingTime n} denotes the existence of $k \ge 1$ such that $\mathrm{iter}(k,n) < n$. The module \texttt{Tail.lean} formalizes the binomial tail count of bad parity words of length $k$, and \texttt{Density.lean} links residue class inclusion to the asymptotic density-one stopping-time statement, while strictly separating Terras density-one finite stopping time from the open global Collatz convergence claim ($\forall n > 0,\,\exists k,\,T^k(n)=1$).
\end{abstract}
